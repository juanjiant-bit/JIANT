# rules on the device (sing.c sy_es), so any Spanish text sings; these words are only what the dice picks from.
# A line: a theme (LOVE NIGHT SEA SKY ROAD DREAM FIRE) or FN (the small words of every theme), a category, words.

#   A adjectives (masculine; -o turns -a after a feminine noun), D determiners (gender-free: mi tu su),
#   P prepositions, C conjunctions. The sound "ny" is the letter enye (suenyo = sueno with a tilde).
