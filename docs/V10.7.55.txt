V10.7.55 beta — V10.7.23 with WEST and readable editing

Base: 841f7dd90cf733c50942ae9292d2e2cb52619bd8 (V10.7.23).
This branch starts from V10.7.23, not the V10.7.54 audio core.

WEST replaces NOISE in engine slot 11, with 20 presets. Legacy NOISE projects
and user presets are converted on load. This conversion approximates their
sounds; NOISE is not bit-identical after conversion. Other common reference
sounds remain unchanged. WEST's initial cost estimate is 4 (like FM6) until
the existing per-track CPU measurement takes over.

The original V10.7.23 punch-ins, voice limits, predictive admission and
downsampling remain in place. Shared-budget reuse prefers the quietest released
tail before the oldest, following Yehnah; held-note and drum protections remain.
See YEHNAH_REFERENCE.txt for attribution, scope and validation. This beta does not include the later
16-voice experimental policy or the later punch-in remapping.

LARGE controls are enabled once on updating preferences. The existing LARGE
setting can subsequently be turned off and that choice is respected. LARGE
menus display four rows with larger lettering; compact mode keeps eight.

Stopped note preview: selecting a piano-roll step, changing its note focus
or pitch, or selecting a drum lane requests a brief audition (about 1/3 s).
The audio thread owns the preview voices. Playback, armed recording, held
keys, incoming MIDI or a page change cancels it. It refuses to compete with
unrelated active voices; it never records, sends MIDI notes or changes the
transport. Previewing a drum step auditions the selected lane. Chord preview
uses the selected track's original voice mode and limits.

TIMELINE is already present in this base, as the first SAVE page. Native
arrangement, parts and clips are retained. REC Q OFF is also already present:
REC + B3 toggles quantization, or use REC Q in the recording controls. Timing
is preserved at the original 1/64-step resolution, not continuous timestamps.

Validation: target size/layout and instruction budgets; reference audio
hashes; WEST extremes and migration; preview ownership, cancellation and
expiry with AddressSanitizer; full UI; recording/microtiming; projects and
presets; threaded mixer; native and WebAssembly audio comparison in CI.
Physical FM-1 listening and worst-case CPU headroom still require beta use.
The public installer and public emulator remain on the stable release policy.
