# CPU history, V10.7.23 → V10.7.42

Juan's question: *what is pushing the CPU to its limit in the last versions? We came from versions that had margin.*

All counts come from **callgrind**, through `tests/cpu_vg.c` and `tests/cpu_vg.py`: x86-64, `gcc -O2`, instructions per sample. They are
exact from run to run, so a difference of 1 is real. Each commit was built in its own git worktree, with the tables
generated by `tools/build.py generate()`.

- **The scenes.** Until V10.7.35, the commits' own `cpu_vg.c` was used, because the current one needs `ENGI_WEST` (WEST replaced
  NOISE in V10.7.36). From V10.7.36 on, the current `cpu_vg.c` was used.
- **What differs between the two.** Only the `eng/NOISE` vs `eng/WEST*` sections. `HOST_LIFE LIFE_DEF` (LIFE at its
  power-on default) is in every `cpu_vg.c` since LIFE exists (9e7f7d6), so each version is measured as it ships.
- **The scenes are the same.** The mix, song, seq, rev, dly and other eng sections run the same scene in every version.

## 3. Inside the sections: V10.7.23 vs V10.7.41, function by function

This is `callgrind_annotate` of `song/cputest`, with `-g` builds, cost per sample.

| Δ / sample | function (file) | cause |
|---|---|---|
| **+94.5** | `mix_block` (fx.c; on the host `bal_block` is inlined there) | **BALANCE**: 2 × 3 one-poles, 4 band gains a side, every sample, on by default (`BAL_DEF` 50). The per-sample loop is ~285 instructions inside loops on pi32v2 (target_budget method, cost 980). |
| +24.5 | `life_master` (life.c, in `macro_master`) | LIFE: the beings, the 6 × 6 weights, each track's offsets, once a block |
| +24.1 | `track_follow_peak` (fx.c) | LIFE asks for a follower on **every live track** (`life.fol`): a peak scan every 4th sample. It read `MC_CPU()` twice a sample (`dx_xon`, `dx_sl`) |
| +21.7 | `life_voice` (life.c, in `voice_block`) | LIFE per voice per block: arc, wander, 1 of 3 noises (`drift_noise` +7.4) |
| +15.9 | `life_ctx`, `life_snd` (life.c, in `mix_part`) | LIFE per part per block: the sends breathing (a search through the matrix's keep list) |
| +16 | `fm6_op_run`, `fm6_render` | LIFE's level breathing and pitch wander on FM6 voices (more operators above threshold, increments recomputed) |
| +6.7 | `voice_block` | LIFE's hook |
| ≈ 0 | everything else | `drum_side_begin` moved from `mix_part` (V10.7.36 refactor, neutral) |

In `mix/idle` (+30): BALANCE's silence check scans the block (+9), and `life_master` still runs its loops with nothing
sounding (+12). LIFE's `mix_part` hooks add +4.5.

### The candidates checked

Each one: does it cost per sample when off, and did it grow since V10.7.23?

- **LIFE.** It costs ~+120, all of it control rate (per block / per voice), plus the 4th-sample followers. With LIFE 0
  none of it runs on the sound: `golden_life0.txt` is bit for bit.
- **The leveler** (`lev_block`, `master_out`). Unchanged since V10.7.23. It costs ~20 / sample of peak scan plus the
  gain. With V10.7.42 it no longer runs on a silent block.
- **EROSION** (`fn_tick`). Once a block, never per sample. At NOISE 0 it keeps the modulator's clock for the F.NOISE
  source and returns. DRUM-X's EROSION filter runs only with `P_DNZ` on. No measurable cost.
- **FREEZE / punch-ins.** `perf_begin` has an early-out, so `perf_block` does not run without a punch-in. Under FREEZE,
  two problems turned up:
  1. **a hardware divide every sample** (`t * (0x80000000u / h)` in `perf_frz`); fixed;
  2. a **bug**: `frz_on` stayed at 1 after FREEZE was let go (see §4); fixed.
- **PROX slew, MATH sync.** Block rate only; no change visible in any section.
- **`voice_soft`.** 2.8 → 3.1 / sample (per block); negligible.
- **The master chain** (TAPE, EQ, DJ FILTER, CLIP). Each is gated: not run when off. Unchanged.
- **The LIVE MIX scope, HOME visual taps.** The audio ISR (`audio.c`) is unchanged since V10.7.23: the scope takes 32
  samples a block. HOME > LIFE reads `life.*`, which the audio side keeps anyway.
- **The per-track meters** (`t->peak` in `mix_part`'s loop). Unchanged.
- **`fx_rest`** (silent buses asleep). It engages: `mix/idle.tail` equals `mix/idle`.

**Conclusion.** No work runs per sample for a feature that is off. All of the +7.7 % comes from two features that are
**on by default**: BALANCE (+3.3 %) and LIFE (+4.2 %).

## 5. What is left: the cost of the two features, and the choices that change the sound

On the same build, `song/cputest`:

| | song/cputest | mix/full | seq/plain | rev/ROOM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V10.7.42 as it ships (BALANCE 50, LIFE 25) | 3075 | 2855 | 2483 | 1484 |
| BALANCE 0 (`BAL_DEF 0`) | 2980 (−95) | 2759 | 2388 | 1388 |
| LIFE 0 | 2987 (−88) | 2779 | 2402 | 1460 |
| both 0 | **2881** (V10.7.23: 2864) | 2683 | 2307 | 1365 |

None of these is applied. They change the sound, so they are the owner's decision. The percentages for 1(b), 1(c),
2(a) and 2(b) are estimates from the profile (the share of the work they halve), not measured:

1. **BALANCE.**
   - (a) Ship it at AMOUNT 0 / off by default: −3.1 %, and a project that turns it on pays for it.
   - (b) Run its split at half the rate (22 kHz: three one-poles a side every 2nd sample, the correction held across
     the pair): about −1.5 %. The bands above ~11 kHz see a held correction, so there would be a tiny difference in the
     top octave.
   - (c) Mid only for the split and the follower, one correction for both sides: about −1.5 %. Wide mixes would be
     corrected a little differently.
2. **LIFE.**
   - (a) The voices' wander and arc every 2nd block (they move over ~0.4 s): about −0.7 %, a slightly coarser wander.
   - (b) LIFE's own followers every 8th sample, not every 4th: about −0.4 %, the follower a little less exact on short
     hits.
   - (c) A lower default LIFE (e.g. 0 with HOME > LIFE to turn it on): −2.9 %.
3. **Device-only.** These cost little on the host count but cost cycles on pi32v2.
   - CLOUD's grain window divides once per grain per sample (`rev_cloud`: `... * 512 / L`). A per-grain reciprocal
     (exact for the grain lengths used) would remove up to CL_N divides a sample. `rev_cloud` is in the RAM code, which
     has 2080 B of headroom, of which 32 B are usable before the 2048 B floor.

1(b) and 2(a) together are estimated to bring CPU TEST to about 3000, with no audible change expected, but not bit for
bit. Both features off brings it to V10.7.23's level.

## 6. V10.7.43: LIFE and BALANCE as the owner decided

The owner's decision (Juan): LIFE is the interaction between the tracks only, the tracks intermodulating each other (no
pitch, no filter, no parameter followed: the matrix does that); BALANCE a light balancer in the background, lower in
amount and in cost. Both stay on by default (LIFE 25, BALANCE 25, was 50).

**Was the cross-FM happening before?** Not at the defaults. `life_pm` ran only with PITCH past 50, and PITCH powers on
at 10: 0 of 2756 blocks of a drone duet, at LIFE 25 and at LIFE 100.

What changed (life.c, fx.c):

- **LIFE**: the voices' wander and arc (`life_voice`), the sends (`life_snd`), the pitch sag, LIFE's own followers scan
  (`life.fol`) and the followers on cutoff and SHAPE (a V10.7.43 beta) are gone. What is left is the cross-FM: up to two
  pairs (one up to LIFE 50), chosen every 8th block by the beings' weights (REACH: the other tracks' share beside the one
  before), the moved track's sum through a 32-sample line driven by the other's samples of the block before (`life_tap`:
  a copy, no scan), ~17 instructions a sample per pair. `life_master` runs its pair search only every 8th block.
- **BALANCE**: one band (the low mids, the difference of two one-poles) a side at an eighth of the rate (the mean of 8
  samples), the correction ramped back at the full rate, the analysis every 4 blocks. Was 2 × 3 one-poles and 4 band
  gains every sample.
- **CLOUD** (bit for bit, all 219 golden renders): each grain read and aged in one visit (was a second pass, `cl_age`),
  and the reads stop past the last grain sounding (`cl_on`, a bit per grain). `cloud_clock` (CLOUD at rest) moved out of
  `rev_idle`'s RAM code: −284 B of RAM code, for +126 B in `rev_cloud`.
- The clean releases (renders/v43-release) cost nothing measurable: `master_in` and the bits below Q15 (`out_fr`, masked
  and cleared in `audio.c`, outside the RAM code).
