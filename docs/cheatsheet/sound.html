NEW IN V10.8.72

FX › OTT

FX › TRACK's fourth page, on every track (a two-band compressor from JIANT BYTE): DEPTH (0 off: no CPU) · TIME · UP/DN (− less lift, + less squash) · X-OVER (the split). LOW and HIGH drawn with their gain; HOME › ECO tags a track with OTT. About 10:1 above −16 dB, up to +30 dB of lift, TIME 56. The generator adds it on the electronic genres. Saved with the project and the user presets.

MATH WAVE SYNC · LOOP

MATH WAVE BYTE · FLOAT · SYNC: on SYNC the formula's rhythm follows the tempo, every key the same pattern transposed. With SYNC, E8 is LOOP: OFF · 4 BAR · 2 BAR · 1 BAR · 1/2 · 1/4 · 1/8 · 1/16 · 1/32, the pattern restarting bar-exact. Preset BYTE SYNC. MOD matrix AMOUNT up to ±200 %.

LIFE STEREO · RESOUND · GEN

NEW IN V10.8.21

DRONE CLAY

SEQ › CLAY: a very low drone in the track's own voice (RATE re-strikes it or holds it) and 4 modulators (SHAPE · EUCLID · AMOUNT · DECAY, gestures ARP CHORD HIT) on any of the track's parameters. Yields the voice to the steps on MONO; a TIME / PITCH punch-in restarts it.

SCREENS REDRAWN

FX HUD (DELAY a tunnel of echoes, REVERB cells, CHORUS L/R orbit), SENDS columns, MIXER channels, ENV layers, LFO satellite, FOCUS heat bar, ARRANGE / GENERATOR a living score. SCL › KEY the scale named whole with its degrees; SCL › CHORD the chord really played, its intervals.

SONG MODE · POWER-ON

HOME › JAM plays A–D per track live, REC a take, LOCK it and play over it (YOU), TAKE edits the changes. Boot "Six threads" with the version; no click at power-on. Removed: STRETCH, MUTATE, SEQ dice, GLOBAL COMP.

NEW IN V10.8.37

EXT · SOUND FROM OUTSIDE

The USB input (IN+OUT, or an interface on the HOST port) on an EXT track: INPUT page SOURCE · GAIN · GATE · MODE, COLOR page WIDTH · RING · BITS · RATE. Then the track's FILTER (the COMBs tuned to the last note), DIST, sends, the SLICER (STUT: a stutter) and the audio punch-ins: REPEAT · REVERSE · FREEZE · TAPE STOP · PITCH UP / DOWN · STRETCH.

WEST DUST · FILTER · DIST · CHORUS

WEST: FOLD SKEW MOD RATIO · GATE DECAY RES DUST. DUST 1–95 white noise folded and gated with the core, 96–127 METAL: a metallic tone tuned to the note. FILTER TYPE: LP · HP · BP · COMB · COMB- (MS20 and LP24 are gone). KEY TRK: OCT+ held + K2 on FILTER, OFF · 50 · 100 · NOTE. ENV › STAGE is gone. FX › DIST K4 NOISE: grain inside the drive (not on AMP). FX › CHORUS K4 MIX: 0 the chorus, 100 a vibrato. CLOUD starts with the note.

DRONE CLAY

DRONE CLAY: CLAY · DRONE · PITCH (−24…+36) · RATE (HOLD, 1/64…4BAR). CLAY MOD: MOD 1–4 · DEST · SHAPE · AMOUNT. CLAY RHYTHM: MOD · RATE · EUCLID · DECAY, a RATE a modulator. CLAY TIME: RUN · SEED. AMOUNT 0 strikes nothing; a bar a modulator under the scope shows what it adds.

NEW IN V10.8.48

LEVEL · GATE

A LEVEL page after EDIT on every engine: GAIN (0–12 dB) · LEVEL · PAN. FX › REVERB and FX › DLY: OCT+ held + K4 = GATE (0 off, 16 = 1/16, 64 = a quarter, 127 = two): the tail cut on time.

PARTS · JAM

PART SLOTS K2 PLAY/EDIT (in on the next bar). SAVE layer SAVE A–D. PLAY mode PART, song end REPEAT. Boxes show n BARS / PLAYING. JAM / TAKE: PART, SWITCH. The ARP on the transport's grid.

GENERATOR

Envelopes by role (leads short sustain, long release), varied filters, parts that move inside each section, sections half as long. RADIO changes every 16 / 8 / 4 bars; STOP on the last bar stops it. Respects SCL › HARMONY.

NEW IN V10.8.42

RESYNC · DJ FILTER · NOTE

SEQ held + PLAY while playing: RESYNC, every track from its step 1 on the next bar (GLO + PLAY restarts at once). GLO › MIXER: a short tap of OCT+ switches K4 between BPM and DJ FILT. SEQ › NOTE is back after STEP; TRANS on SCL › CHORD K4. Gone: SEQ › BAR, SEQ › PHRASES, GLO › SYSTEM 2.

GAIN · KEY TRK · SWARM

GAIN 0–12 dB on every engine: first EDIT page, OCT+ held + K4. KEY TRK on FILTER: OCT+ held + K2, OFF · 50 · 100 · NOTE. SWARM: WAVE on SWARM 2 K4, a tag ALONE / AIR n / PROX n, MODE >T1…>T6 plays that track's notes.

GENERATOR · RADIO · MOOD

GENERATOR 4: K3 RADIO ON / OFF (the next piece by itself when a song ends, or after 16 bars of a loop; OCT+ starts it), K4 MOOD CALM … DANCE (the energy, the drums, the choice of genres on RANDOM). 29 genres (FUGA is gone). The tempo exact and the tracks always in phase.

SEQUENCER

64 steps a track, up to 4 notes a step, piano roll and drum grid, four sequences A–D (STEP K1). One chain: STEP · NOTE · LENGTH · RHYTHM · TRACK · EUCLID · MODES · PLAYHEADS · STEP FX · STEP FX 2 · AUTOMATION · AUTO LIST · LYRIC STEP · TRACK GEN · DRONE CLAY · CLAY MOD · CLAY RHYTHM · CLAY TIME; PRESETS picks the step. Hold a step + turn a knob: a lock. STEP K4 VEL: 1–127, then ACC, SLD, A+S. SEQ EDIT (SEQ held): a selection, an ACTION (STRETCH SQUEEZE REPEAT NUDGE ROTATE SHIFT REVERSE DUP COPY PASTE CLEAR CLR AUTO CLR STEP VEL RND NOTE RND HUMANIZE TRANSP OCTAVE THIN INVERT STUTTER ARP MUTATE GEN RANDOM), OCT+ does it, F#5 UNDO / REDO. STEP FX: two components a step, ACTION · LENGTH · COND · VALUE: ARP SEQ OCT+ OCT− RATCHET STUTTER MUTE TRANS RANDOM FILL RAMP SOUND BEND SHIFT CHORD TRIG; COND ALWAYS, 25 / 50 / 75 %, 1ST, FILL, PRE, NEI (and their !), N:M, ELSE. On STEP FX the white keys play components live (REC writes them); black 1–9 LIVE. EUCLID: HITS STEPS ROTATE and ROLE (MELODY CHORDS BASS ARP; DRUM-X: DENSITY and LANE); OCT+ mutates, OCT− the anchor, OCT+ held + K4 EVERY. TRACK: PITCH, CHANCE, HUMAN, REC GRID. REC + knobs while playing: automation.

SCALE · ARP

ROOT and SCALE are every melodic track's: CHR MAJ MIN DOR MIX PEN MPEN HARM PHRY LYD LOC MEL BLUES WHOLE DIMHW DIMWH. QUANT snaps keys or the sequence, ALL TRK moves every sequence along the scale. CHORD: a key plays a chord, VOICING, STRUM, TRANS. HARMONY: PROG LENGTH EVOLVE SKIP, the tracks follow a progression (roman numerals on screen). SCENE: each track a role and one key plays them all.

ARP MODES

OFF UP DN UPDN RND ORD REPEAT DNUP UP+8 CONV DIVG PINKY THUMB WALK CHORD TRNS (the keys transpose the sequence). The ARP runs on the transport's grid.

SONGS

6 projects × 4 parts (A–D). HOME › SONG: JAM plays each track's A–D live (F3–B3, OCT+ GO, OCT− STOP), REC a take, K4 LOCK plays it and you play over it (YOU); TAKE edits its changes; ARRANGE the song as up to 16 rows (part and bars, a scene each). SAVE held: PLAY A–D on the next bar, SAVE A–D (OVERWRITE A? over music), SONG REC. SAVE › PART SLOTS (PLAY/EDIT, SAVE) and PROJECTS. Each part keeps its sounds, steps, automation, kit, SLICER, OTT and macros. JIANT Studio lists, renames, copies and moves projects.
