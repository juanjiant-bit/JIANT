NEW IN V10.8.72

SEQ EDIT (SEQ HELD)

Replaces SEQ TOOLS. The roll with the selection: K1 START · K2 END (A3: MOVE / WIDTH) · K3 ACTION · K4 its amount; OCT+ / D5 DO IT on the selection, or on all steps (OCT− / G3 ALL). ACTIONs: STRETCH SQUEEZE REPEAT NUDGE ROTATE SHIFT REVERSE DUP COPY PASTE CLEAR CLR AUTO CLR STEP VEL RND NOTE RND HUMANIZE TRANSP OCTAVE THIN INVERT STUTTER ARP MUTATE GEN RANDOM. B3–E4 BAR 1–4, C#5 / D#5 NUDGE, F#5 UNDO / REDO. Step keys held: a range.

RESOUND · SAVE · UNDO

GLO + A4 RESOUND: new sounds for every track by its role, the notes and the song kept. SAVE over a part with music asks OVERWRITE A? (OCT+ yes, OCT− no). GLO + G4 UNDO takes back a RESOUND, a save or a SEQ EDIT action (again: redo).

HOME · MENU

HOME › ECOSYSTEM · LIFE · MACROS (K1–K4 M1–M4, HOME + SELECT DICE MACROS). LIFE K3 STEREO (was PUMP). HOME › BEING · VISUAL: K1 DENSITY · K2 WOBBLE · K3 PALETTE · K4 REACT, OCT+ a new one. MENU › DISPLAY › SAVER (OFF 1 3 5 10 MIN) and PANEL (AUTO / A / B; OCT− + SAVE at power-on: A).

NEW IN V10.8.21

HOME › SONG · JAM · TAKE

JAM: F3–B3 pick A–D on every track, OCT+ GO, OCT− STOP. REC records the take; K4 LOCK plays it, what you play over it is YOU; a track picked + REC redoes it one round, OCT− undoes. TAKE: K1 pick · K2 move · K3 SEQ · K4 BARS, OCT+ new, OCT− delete.

SEQ › STEP FX · CLAY

STEP FX: PRESETS picks the step; the 16 white keys play components while held (STUTTER ÷1 ÷2 ÷4 · RATCHET x2 x3 x4 x8 · ARP ↑ ↓ ↕ · OCTAVE ± · RANDOM · TRANSPOSE +2 · CHORD · MUTE), with REC written into the step. DRONE CLAY: a low drone + 4 modulators per track (DRONE CLAY · CLAY MOD 1–4 · CLAY DECAY · CLAY TIME).

TRACK BAR · MACROS · SCREENS

Top bar: TRACK N / ENGINE in the track's colour (T1 yellow … T6 blue). LFO › MACROS: K1 the macro, its destinations add up (16 routes); M1–M4 are MOD destinations. A big banner names a held key. Gone: GLO LIVE, STRETCH, MUTATE, SEQ dice, GLOBAL COMP, ENV TRACKS.

NEW IN V10.8.37

SOUND IN · EXT

MENU › SYSTEM › USB › IN+OUT (computer / phone) or HOST (a USB audio interface) brings audio in; a track with the EXT engine plays it: SOURCE IN 1 · IN 2 · 1+2 · STEREO, GAIN, GATE, MODE THRU / KEYS (the keys and steps gate it), RING, BITS, RATE. MOD source IN ENV: the input moves any parameter.

LIVE · MUTATE · EUCLID

SEQ › STEP FX, black keys 1–9 on the selected track: RATE x2 · x3/2 · x2/3 · REVERSE · PING-PONG · RANDOM · THIN held, GIRO − / + pressed. MUTATE: EUCLID, OCT+ held + K4 = EVERY (OFF 1 2 4 8 bars, a tag in the picture); on EUCLID OCT+ mutates now, OCT− the anchor. EUCLID K4 ROLE on a melodic track; its MODES page is MELODY.

DRONE CLAY KEYS · GONE

CLAY ON: the white keys strike gestures on the MOD picked (OCT− OCT+ the group), the last seven keys the drone's degree; RUN SONG NOKEY / FREE NOKEY: the keys play notes. Pages: DRONE CLAY · CLAY MOD · CLAY RHYTHM · CLAY TIME. Gone: filter MS20 and LP24, ENV › STAGE, the PUNCH lane page.

NEW IN V10.8.48

LEVEL · GATE

A LEVEL page after EDIT on every engine: GAIN (0–12 dB) · LEVEL · PAN. FX › REVERB and FX › DLY: OCT+ held + K4 = GATE (0 off, 16 = 1/16, 64 = a quarter, 127 = two): the tail cut on time.

PARTS · JAM

PART SLOTS K2 PLAY/EDIT (in on the next bar). SAVE layer SAVE A–D. PLAY mode PART, song end REPEAT. Boxes show n BARS / PLAYING. JAM / TAKE: PART, SWITCH. The ARP on the transport's grid.

GENERATOR

Envelopes by role (leads short sustain, long release), varied filters, parts that move inside each section, sections half as long. RADIO changes every 16 / 8 / 4 bars; STOP on the last bar stops it. Respects SCL › HARMONY.

NEW IN V10.8.42

RESYNC · DJ FILTER · NOTE

SEQ held + PLAY while playing: RESYNC, every track from its step 1 on the next bar (GLO + PLAY restarts at once). GLO › MIXER: a short tap of OCT+ switches K4 between BPM and DJ FILT. SEQ › NOTE is back after STEP; TRANS on SCL › CHORD K4. Gone: SEQ › BAR, SEQ › PHRASES, GLO › SYSTEM 2.

GAIN · KEY TRK · SWARM

GAIN 0–12 dB on every engine: first EDIT page, OCT+ held + K4. KEY TRK on FILTER: OCT+ held + K2, OFF · 50 · 100 · NOTE. SWARM: WAVE on SWARM 2 K4, a tag ALONE / AIR n / PROX n, MODE >T1…>T6 plays that track's notes.

GENERATOR · RADIO · MOOD

GENERATOR 4: K3 RADIO ON / OFF (the next piece by itself when a song ends, or after 16 bars of a loop; OCT+ starts it), K4 MOOD CALM … DANCE (the energy, the drums, the choice of genres on RANDOM). 29 genres (FUGA is gone). The tempo exact and the tracks always in phase.

NAVIGATION · SIX RULES

1 · SELECT WALKS THE PAGES

Every page of the section in one chain, a page a detent, stopping at both ends. No levels, no zoom.

FX: SENDS → DIST → AUTO PAN → OTT → BUSES → DLY → … → EQ

2 · THE BUTTON: IN, OR ON

From another section: the page you were last on there. Already inside: the first page of the next group (round).

FX › DLY 2: FX → REVERB · EDIT › FILTER: EDIT → FOCUS

3 · PRESETS LOADS SOUNDS

Always the track's sounds (factory and user), GEN's pages too. On SEQ pages it picks the step (slow a step, fast a bar). Never a page, a track or a module.

FX › DLY: PRESETS ▶ the next sound

4 · KNOB 1 PICKS

The slot, the stage, the operator, the kit's sound, the row, the macro. The track: always ALGORITHM.

LFO › MOD: K1 = the slot · SOUND: K1 = the kit's sound

5 · HOLD = THE LAYER

Held + SELECT: HOME the GENERATOR · EDIT the sound dice · GLO the tempo · the others walk their chain.

SEQ + G5 CLR STEP · SAVE + C5 NAME

6 · THE SCREEN SAYS WHERE

Tabs on top: the section's groups, the one shown lit. Footer: the page and n/N; a knob turned is said whole with its page.

FX · DELAY · DLY 2 7/20 · "LFO RATE 1.24 HZ"

THE PANEL

KNOBS AND TRANSPORT

MASTERMaster volume.
SELECTWalks the section's pages, a page a detent, stopping at the ends (the MENU: every row). Held with a button: HOME the GENERATOR (on HOME › MACROS: DICE MACROS); EDIT the sound dice; GLO the tempo; the others walk their chain.
PRESETSAlways sounds (factory and user) on every page; on SEQ pages the step (slow a step, fast a bar). Nothing in the MENU or with a button held, except EDIT: the first sound of the next / previous engine.
ALGORITHMSelects the track from any page: T1–T4, or T1–T6 with TRACKS 6. MENU: the tabs.
KNOB 1–4The four cards on screen. Fast sweeps the whole range, slow fine-tunes. With a layer held: the layer's. A step held: a parameter lock.
OCT− · OCT+The keyboard's octave (both together: back to 0); on DRUM the kit stays put and OCT picks the KEY MAP's rows. On action pages and dialogs: back / confirm. OCT+ held + a knob: the page's hidden one (GAIN, KEY TRK, GATE, EVERY).
PLAYStart / stop. GLO + PLAY: restart from the top. SEQ + PLAY while playing: RESYNC on the next bar.
RECArms live recording on the selected track (notes and knob moves, overdub).
HOMETap: HOME (on HOME the next group: ECO · BEING · SONG · GENE). Hold: the MENU.
KEYS27 keys play the selected track in its scale; with a layer held they become that layer's.
