# Parameter names: context, not new words (V10.8.64, V10.8.65)

The owner: "nombres universales como RATE, FREQUENCY, PITCH, TONE, DECAY, que son cualidades del sonido y su timbre,
está bien que se repitan; el problema es que no se ve que dice LIFE y la gente no entiende que está paneando".

## Kept as they are (universal sound qualities)

RATE, FREQ, PITCH, TONE, DECAY, ATTACK, RELEASE, CUTOFF, RES, LEVEL, PAN, MIX, DEPTH, GAIN, DRIVE, SPREAD.
The same word means the same quality wherever it appears; the page it is on says whose it is.

## DIST TYPE's names (V10.8.72)

DIST TYPE keeps its first six at their stored numbers: 0 SOFT, 1 HARD, 2 FOLD (FM's triangle wavefolder), 3 CRUSH,
4 RECT, 5 AMP. JIANT BYTE's formula effects follow, 6 RING, 7 STEPS, 8 XOR, 9 WOB, 10 SFOLD, 11 SAT, with BYTE's
names (BYTE's GATE, LPF, LFO.F, HPF and RND are not in FM: FM has its own gate and filters). SFOLD (a sine fold) is a different sound from FOLD, so the two names stay
apart; SAT is BYTE's saturator (a tanh, its level made up), not SOFT's biased clip.

## Renamed (ambiguous, not a sound quality)

| Old | New | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LIFE KNOB 3 "PUMP" | "STEREO" | the mode itself changed: tracks move across the stereo field, pushed by their neighbours (was a level duck) |

No stored id, parameter order, NRPN number or editor-protocol id changed.

## Reviewed, not renamed (V10.8.65)

MODE, TYPE, AMOUNT, SYNC, MOTION and DICE stay. Each one always appears on a page (or a banner) that names its module, and
the knob line now says that module with it ("LFO MODE TRIG", "DIST TYPE SOFT", "MOD AMOUNT 40", "DICE MACROS"), so the
same word no longer reads as the same control. A new word would only move the ambiguity to the manual and to Studio.

ENV, LFO, MOD, MSEQ, the FX lens pictures (SENDS, DIST, AUTO PAN, BUSES, DELAY, REVERB, CHORUS, SLICER, MASTER),
SCL (KEY, CHORD, HARMONY, SCENE), the SEQ roll pages, EDIT's creatures, SYSTEM, MIXER, PRESETS, SAVE's pages and HOME's
ECO / BEING / VISUAL: their picture fills the panel; the name stays in the footer.
