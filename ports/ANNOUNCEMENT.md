## English

**JIANT FM leaves the FM-1 🎹**

JIANT FM is no longer just for the M-VAVE FM-1. The same firmware, note for note, now runs on:

- **Akai MPC Sample**: JIANT as the whole instrument, with its screen, pads, knobs and wheel. It boots from the
  microSD card.
  Download: https://github.com/juanjiant-bit/JIANT/releases · How to install: https://github.com/juanjiant-bit/JIANT/blob/main/ports/mpc-sample/README.md ·
  Emulator: https://juanjiant-bit.github.io/JIANT/emu/devices.html#mpc · Editor: coming.
- **M-VAVE SMK-37 Pro / Elite**: in development, on the same chip as the FM-1. Install: not yet (build from source:
  https://github.com/juanjiant-bit/JIANT/blob/main/docs/SMK37.md) · Emulator: https://juanjiant-bit.github.io/JIANT/emu/devices.html#smk37 ·
  Editor: https://juanjiant-bit.github.io/JIANT/webapp/editor/
- And the **M-VAVE FM-1**, as always. Installer: https://juanjiant-bit.github.io/JIANT/webapp/installer/ ·
  Emulator: https://juanjiant-bit.github.io/JIANT/emu/index.html · Editor: https://juanjiant-bit.github.io/JIANT/webapp/editor/

On the bigger CPUs, JIANT goes further than the FM-1:

- **SAMPLE**: an experimental one-shot sampler with LOOP, PING, REV, SLICE, SCRUB and TAPE modes. It has RATCHET,
  ROUND-ROBIN, CHANCE and LEGATO triggers, a resonant feedback, DESTROY (each hit wears the sample down) and
  resampling.
- **GRAIN**: granular synthesis with FWD, REV, PING, RANDOM, STRETCH and FREEZE travel. It has feedback with shimmer
  and a LIVE mode that granulates whatever is playing.
- **DRUM-X with samples**: a morph between the synthesized drums and your samples.
- Every track gets its own 8 voices, without the FM-1's CPU limits.

**Try it now in the browser**, each device with its own panel. On the MPC you can drop in your own WAVs:
https://juanjiant-bit.github.io/JIANT/emu/devices.html

**Downloads (preview):** https://github.com/juanjiant-bit/JIANT/releases

⚠️ This is a preview: nothing has been tried on real hardware yet. **We're looking for testers** with an MPC Sample
or an SMK-37. Tell us how it goes in Issues or here.

Free and open, GPL-3.0. 🧡
